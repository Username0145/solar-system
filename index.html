Welcome to our Solar System

The Solar System is in a large universe. Our solar system is located in the Milky Way. It includes the Sun, eight planets, 5 dwarf planets, hundreds of moons, and thousands of asteroids and comets. The Solar System formed about 4.6 billion years ago when a molecular cloud collapsed, creating our Sun.
