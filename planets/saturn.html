Saturn's facts

  • Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun, about 876.27 million miles away.
  • It is the second largest planet, about 9 times wider than Earth with an equatorial diameter of about 74,897 miles.
  • A day on Saturn is about 11 hours long.
  • One year on Saturn takes about 29.4 to 29.5 Earth years or 10,756 days.
  • Saturn has a density of 0.687 g/cm³
  • Saturn rings are extensive and have complex ring system. The rings are made of water ice, with a trace component of rocky material.
  • Saturn's average temperature is around -220°F (-140°C)
  • Saturn has hundreds of moons in its orbit.
