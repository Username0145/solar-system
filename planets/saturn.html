Saturn: Lord of the Rings

Saturn is famous for its beautiful rings

Fun Facts about Saturn

  • Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun, about 1.4 billion km away.
  • It is the second largest planet, about 9 times wider than Earth.
  • A day on Saturn is about 10.7 hours long.
  • One year on Saturn takes about 29 Earth years.
  • Saturn is less dense than water, so it would float in a big enough bathtub.
  • Its rings are made mostly of water ice and are very thin for their size.
  • Saturn has well over 100 known moons.
  • Its moon Titan is bigger than the planet Mercury.
