Uranus is an ice giant that spins on its side

Fun Facts about Uranus

  • Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun.
  • Uranus' distance from the sun is about 1.8062 billion miles.
  • Uranus' mass is about 86.8 septillion kilograms, roughly 14.5 times the mass of earth.
  • has 29 oficially recognized moons, including 5 major moons: Miranda, Ariel, Umbriel, Titania, and Oberon.
  • 42 years of continuous sunlight and darkness for either side
  • Tilted at an angle of roughly 98 degrees. Roatates on its side.
  • Uranus have rings, composed of water ice and processed organics.
  • Uranus is blue-green due to the presence of methan in their atmospheres.
