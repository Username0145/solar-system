What is the Earth?

The Earth is the only planet with living things

Fun Facts about the Earth

  • Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the fifth largest planet.
  • The only place we know that Earth is inhabited by living things.
  • Earth is the only planet in the solar system whose English name does not come from Greek or Roman mythology. The neame was taken from Old English and Germanic. It means "the ground."
  • Earth's life begin about 3.8 billion years ago.
  • One complete rotation is every 23.9 hourss
  • Takes 365.25 days to complete one trip around the Sun.
  • Earth is composed of four main layers; inner core, outer core, mantle, and crust.

Why is the Earth important

Earth is the only world in the solar system with liquid surface water and an oxygen-rich atmosphere capable of sustaining a rich biosphere
