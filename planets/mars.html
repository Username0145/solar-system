Mars: The Red Planet

Mars gets its red color from rusty iron in its soil

Mars Facts

  • Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun, about 228 million km away.
  • It is 6,779 km wide, about half the size of Earth.
  • A day on Mars is 24 hours and 37 minutes. A year is 687 Earth days.
  • Mars has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos.
  • The average temperature is about -63°C (-81°F).
  • Olympus Mons is the tallest volcano in the solar system, about 22 km high.
  • Valles Marineris is a canyon more than 4,000 km long.
  • Mars looks red because its dust contains iron oxide, or rust.

Read More About Mars

Olympus Mons is an extinct volcano about 22 km tall, nearly three times the height of Mount Everest.

Mars once had rivers and lakes. Today most of its water is frozen in the polar ice caps and underground.

NASA's Perseverance rover landed in Jezero Crater in 2021 to search for signs of ancient life and collect rock samples.
