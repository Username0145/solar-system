Mars: The Red Planet

Mars gets its red color from rusty iron in its soil

Mars Facts

  • Mars has a average distance of 228 million km from the Sun.
  • The temperatur on Mars is -153°C to 20°C.
  • Mars has 2 moons.
  • Mars is a terrestrial planet.
  • The size of planet Mars is about 21,000 km.

Read More About Mars

Olympus Mons is a large volcano, and the tallest mountain in the solar system. Named after the mythological home of the greek gods. Olympus Mons is almost three times taller than Mount Everest and roughly the size of the state of Arizona.

Mars once had rivers and lakes. Today scientists has discovered complex water activity on rocks underground.

NASA's Perseverance rover landed in Jezero Crater in 2021 to search for signs of ancient life and collect rock samples. Then on Sepetember 2023, the Rover founded complex water underground. Called the "Margin Unit".
